Answers to some common questions

Can I invite you to photograph my event?

You absolutely can, I will let you know whether I'm available on your dates and the event is a good match for my style.

Do you sell your photos?

Sure do! Drop me an email or a DM with the photos or event you are interested in and I will let you know what I can offer. Some of my photos aren't suitable for printing, but if that is the case I will let you know.

Do you have photos of me at other events?

Possibly! Email or DM me with your details and I will see what else I can find.
